ABOUT KAAMCONNECT

Connecting skills to opportunity. Empowering workers through accessible technology.

WHAT IS KAAMCONNECT?

KaamConnect is a digital employment platform designed for daily wage and semi-skilled workers who are often excluded from traditional online job systems.

Millions of workers possess valuable skills but lack digital literacy, verified identity, or reliable access to employers. KaamConnect removes these barriers using voice technology, simplified workflows, and community verification.

THE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING

  • ❌ Informal hiring leads to exploitation and unpaid work
  • ❌ Workers struggle with complex apps and forms
  • ❌ Employers cannot find trusted local workers
  • ❌ Lack of verification reduces safety and trust

WHAT WE HAVE ACHIEVED

  • ✔ Voice-based worker registration for low-literacy users
  • ✔ AI-assisted job matching based on skill and location
  • ✔ Worker & Employer role-based dashboards
  • ✔ Public job discovery without login barriers
  • ✔ Secure authentication and profile management
  • ✔ Inclusive multilingual platform design

NGO & COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIPS

KaamConnect is built with a community-first approach. We envision partnerships with NGOs and local organizations to verify workers, support onboarding, and build trust between employers and workers.

NGOs can act as verification partners, helping workers who may not have formal documentation gain access to digital employment opportunities.

OUR IMPACT VISION

Our goal is not just job matching — it is economic empowerment.

  • ✔ Reduce unemployment in informal sectors
  • ✔ Enable digital inclusion for rural workers
  • ✔ Improve worker safety and payment security
  • ✔ Build a trusted labor ecosystem

WHAT'S NEXT
